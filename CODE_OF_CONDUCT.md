# Code of conduct

Carnival Arcade is a place to build and play together. Everyone who takes part
in this repository, in its issues and pull requests, follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient with people who are learning, and assume good
intent. Harassment, personal attacks and unwelcome behavior of any kind aren't
OK here.

## Report a problem

Contact the repository's maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/xinyuliew). If the problem involves
something posted here, you can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on
the comment, issue or pull request. Say what happened, where and when, and who
was involved.

The maintainer decides what happens next, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
